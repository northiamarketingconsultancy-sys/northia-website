# How to Find the Right Asian KOL List for Your Brand in Canada and North America

Find and screen Asian, Chinese and local influencers by city, language, audience and customer fit—not follower count alone.

**People:** Marketing managers, founders, restaurant owners, retail operators and local campaign teams
**Industry:** Food and beverage, beauty, retail, lifestyle, finance and local services
**Scenario:** The brand needs an Asian KOL list but does not know which creators can reach its actual customers
**Application:** Use before a store opening, product launch, tasting, event or seasonal promotion

## What an Asian KOL list should contain

A useful list includes creator name, city, platform, content language, audience location, topic, typical views, engagement quality, contact route, expected fee and brand fit. A long spreadsheet without these fields is only a directory.

## Start with the customer, not the creator

Define the customer’s city, age, language, interests, buying reason and visit radius. A Vancouver food creator and a Toronto beauty creator may both be Asian, but they solve completely different campaign needs.

## Where to find Asian and Chinese influencers

Search Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Xiaohongshu using category, city and language combinations. Review tagged locations, competitor mentions, event guest lists and creators already discussing the customer problem.

## Check audience location and language

Ask for recent audience insights showing top cities and countries. Review comment language and local references. A creator can live in Canada while most followers remain overseas, which may not help a local launch.

## Look beyond follower count

Compare normal views across several posts, relevant comments, saves, posting consistency and past brand work. Sudden spikes, repeated generic comments and weak local interaction can signal poor fit or unreliable reach.

## Choose the right collaboration type

Paid content offers clearer deliverables. Gifting works when the product is relevant and there is no posting guarantee. Tastings and events suit local experiences. Agree on usage rights, disclosure, timing and reporting before launch.

## A simple shortlist process

Build a broad list, filter by city and audience, score brand fit, review content safety, contact the strongest candidates and keep backups. The final shortlist should explain why each KOL fits—not simply rank creators by size.
