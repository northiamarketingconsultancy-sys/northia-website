## What the evidence says

AnyMind’s 2026 APAC study analyzed nearly 7,000 campaigns and more than 1.1 million influencers across 10 markets. It reports a shift toward performance-led influencer activity, with micro and nano creators contributing more than 40% of impact in its dataset.

## People this guide is for

Niche local communities, high-intent creator followers, diaspora audiences and repeat-participation customer groups.

## Application scenarios

Use large KOLs when broad awareness and cultural signaling are the job. Use micro or nano creators when niche trust, local relevance, product demonstration or community participation matters. Mix tiers when the campaign needs both legitimacy and conversion.

## What to measure

Connect creator activity to the job: qualified discovery, saves and search lift for consideration; clicks, bookings or sales for conversion; opt-ins, repeat participation and referrals when the goal is community growth.
