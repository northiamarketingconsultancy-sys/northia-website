Who we are

Most cross-border marketing rents attention: ads that stop the moment the budget stops, influencers who post once and disappear. We build the opposite — owned resonance. A brand positioned to mean something in its new market, surrounded by a community of creators and customers who advocate because they belong, not because they were paid to shout.

The Northia System

One method. Three stages.
Diagnose → Rebrand → Activate.

A brand wins a new market in three moves: understand what it means there, rebuild what it should mean, then make the market say it for you. Every engagement has explicit decision gates, so nothing advances on vibes.

00 · DIAGNOSE — FREE

The Resonance Audit

Five MECE dimensions, scored against your target market — the diagnostic that tells you where your budget should go before you spend it.

GOVERNING QUESTIONWhat does your brand currently mean in this market — and to whom?
D1

Positioning Fit

Does it solve a problem this market actually has?

D2

Language & Message Fit

Does it read native — or imported?

D3

Trust Signals

Does it show what this market checks before buying?

D4

Channel–Buyer Fit

Is it present where these buyers research?

D5

Community Ownership

Does anyone advocate for it — unpaid?

Routing logic: weak on D1–D3 → start with The Rebrand. Weak on D4–D5 → start with The Community Activation Engine. Weak on both → the full system, in sequence. Strong across all five → you don't need us yet, and we'll say so.
