# What Chef Fei’s First U.S. Restaurant Teaches Chinese F&B Brands About North American Location Strategy in 2026

A practical 2026 location strategy for Chinese restaurant brands choosing their first U.S. or Canadian site.

**People:** Chinese restaurant founders, overseas expansion leaders, franchise teams and local marketers
**Industry:** Restaurant chains, food and beverage, franchising and mall dining
**Scenario:** A proven brand in China is selecting its first U.S. or Canadian city and trade area
**Application:** Use before signing a lease, choosing a launch market or committing a major opening budget

## The first location is a positioning decision

Chef Fei opened its first U.S. restaurant at Westfield UTC in San Diego in September 2026. The centre already houses established Asian dining names such as Din Tai Fung and Haidilao. For a Chinese restaurant brand entering North America, the first address influences who discovers the brand, what customers compare it with and whether the launch feels credible.

## Choose the trade area, not only the city

A city-level population number is too broad for a lease decision. Map a realistic drive-time area and compare weekday workers, nearby households, students, tourists, income, cuisine familiarity and evening traffic. The right city can still contain the wrong block.

## Diaspora beachhead or mainstream traffic

An Asian commercial district can offer cultural familiarity and faster word of mouth. A mainstream mall can provide broader discovery and stronger institutional trust, but may require clearer menu education and higher occupancy costs. Decide which audience must validate the first unit.

## Use the cluster effect carefully

Nearby Asian restaurant leaders can confirm that customers already travel to the area for Asian dining. They also raise the comparison standard. Review price, wait time, group-dining capacity, parking, service expectations and the occasions competitors already own.

## Rent and footfall are not enough

Model lunch, dinner, weekend, delivery and private-event demand separately. Check ventilation, kitchen capacity, licensing, labour access, supply routes and landlord restrictions. A visible site that cannot support the operating model is not a strong market-entry site.

## Validate demand before signing

Run geo-targeted Xiaohongshu and Instagram content, a local waitlist page, community tastings and delivery-radius tests. Track qualified sign-ups, menu questions, driving distance and intended visit occasions rather than impressions alone.

## A 30/60/90-day location plan

Days 1–30: shortlist cities and trade areas using customer and operational evidence. Days 31–60: test positioning, menu and local demand. Days 61–90: negotiate only the sites that pass the scorecard, then build search, creator and community activity around the chosen opening zone.
