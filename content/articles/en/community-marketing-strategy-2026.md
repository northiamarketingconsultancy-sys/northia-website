## What this guide is for

Use when paid media or creator campaigns generate attention but the brand has no repeat relationship with the audience.

## Why community is different

Community marketing is not simply posting more often. It creates a repeatable reason for customers, creators or members to return, participate, contribute and recommend—so the relationship can continue after paid reach ends.

## People this guide is for

Existing customers, repeat buyers, members, advocates and high-intent prospects who can become part of an owned brand community.

## Application scenarios

For restaurants, build recurring tables, local groups or ambassador mechanics. For beauty and lifestyle, use creator cohorts, product education and member access. For services, create useful recurring information, peer proof and events around a shared problem.

## What to measure

Measure owned audience growth, participation, repeat attendance or purchase, referrals, user-generated content and the share of demand that returns without being repurchased through media.

## Northia application framework

1. Define the exact market and community.
2. Translate the buying reason and proof.
3. Match discovery channels to buyer behaviour.
4. Create local proof before scaling reach.
5. Measure the next behaviour toward purchase, retention or advocacy.
