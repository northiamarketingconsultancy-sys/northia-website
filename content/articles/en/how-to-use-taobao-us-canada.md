# As a Non-Chinese Speaker, How Can I Use Taobao in the US and Canada?

Non-Chinese speakers in the United States and Canada can use Taobao by downloading the app, creating an account, using English or image search, learning a small set of Chinese checkout labels and selecting an available international shipping method. Shipping eligibility, interface options and final cost vary by country, product and account.

## Who is this guide for?

- **People:** Non-Chinese-speaking shoppers, creators, event organizers and small-business owners.
- **Industry:** Cross-border e-commerce, custom printing, events, décor, fashion and craft supplies.
- **Scenario:** You want Taobao's wider product choice but cannot confidently read the Chinese app or shipping labels.
- **Application:** Use in the United States or Canada to search, compare, ship and pay with fewer avoidable mistakes.

## Can you use Taobao without speaking Chinese?

Yes. English searches can return relevant products. The Mainland China setting may show a wider catalogue, but it also introduces more Chinese interface text and prices may display in CNY.

## Setup and search

1. Download the official Taobao app.
2. Review permissions and register using an available method.
3. Decide whether to keep the local English-facing setup or explore the Mainland China setting.
4. Search with English terms or use image search.
5. Compare listings, seller history, buyer photos, dimensions, materials and delivery eligibility.

## What do the shipping options mean?

These delivery examples came from a September 2026 walkthrough and are not permanent promises.

| Option | Meaning | Example timing |
|---|---|---|
| 直邮－空运 / Direct air | Direct overseas delivery by air; easiest and fastest, often most expensive | 10–12 business days |
| 集运－空运 / Consolidated air | Orders go to a China warehouse, are combined, then sent by air | 8–10 business days after consolidation |
| 直邮－海运 / Direct sea | Direct overseas delivery by sea; cheaper but slower | 38–40 days |
| 集运－海运 / Consolidated sea | Orders are combined at a China warehouse, then sent by sea | 37–47 days after consolidation |
| 联系卖家发货 / Arrange with seller | Buyer contacts the seller to arrange cross-border delivery | Varies |

Consolidation or forwarding may involve a second freight payment, warehouse handling, duties, taxes or carrier fees.

## What is worth buying?

Taobao can be useful for custom printed materials, packaging, booth materials, event props, furniture and home décor, fabrics, craft materials, clothing and accessories.

Beginners should be more cautious with food and electronic devices because of possible import restrictions, batteries, compatibility, certifications, warranties and special freight requirements.
